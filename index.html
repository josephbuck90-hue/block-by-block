Block by Block

Six crews, one city. Pick your gang, take the southwest corner, and build an operation on cash from the neighborhood businesses. Upgrade your buildings to unlock heavier hitters, then take down every rival Hideout. Keep your heat down. Push it past the line and squads roll out of the police station downtown.

Paused

Runners collect cash at businesses and bring it to your Hideout or a Front. Every trip, shot and takedown adds heat. A Front cools heat faster. At 70 heat, squads leave the police station and hunt your crew. Select a building to upgrade it and unlock new units.

Multiplayer games keep running while this menu is open.

Campaign

Sixth Street starts with one corner. Take the city one crew at a time. Each win unlocks the next job.
