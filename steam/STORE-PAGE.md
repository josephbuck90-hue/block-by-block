## About this game

**The city is up for grabs.** Concrete Empire: Gangs of the Block is a real-time strategy game set on the rain-slick streets of a modern American city. Pick one of six crews, send your Runners to shake down the neighborhood businesses, and turn that cash into Clubhouses, Armories, Chop Shops and an army that can take the whole city.

**Build your operation**
- Collect from bodegas, barbershops, pawn shops and the Trap House, or work the Stroll after dark
- Upgrade your buildings and research better bags, laundering and protection
- Hide your crew inside buildings, set up Lookouts and guard your stash

**Take the streets**
- Six gangs, each with its own signature unit, gang power, colors, mural and music
- Veterans rank up from Veteran to Elite to Legend and hit harder
- Hold a block and your tag goes up on the walls, and that turf pays you every second

**Watch your heat**
- Every job draws attention. Run too hot and squads roll out of the police station downtown
- City events keep every match moving: armored trucks to rob, parades, blackouts, crackdowns, truces and paydays

**A city that feels lived in**
- Hand-built 3D characters and vehicles, cracked streets, graffiti, burned-out neon and battle scars that stay on the block
- Rain, fog and a full day-night cycle
- Homeless camps, stray dogs, parked cars and traffic that pulls over at the curb

**Play your way**
- A six-mission campaign and a guided tutorial
- 9 game modes: Conquest, Turf Control, Cash Race, King of the Hill, Timed, Regicide, Heist, Survival and Sandbox, plus Teams and Hardcore rules
- Online multiplayer for up to 6 players, co-op against the AI and built-in voice chat
- Five city layouts and random starting spots every map
- Fully playable in English and Spanish

## Mature content survey (answer these in Steamworks → Content Survey)
- **Frequent violence or gore:** Yes. Constant stylized gang combat with guns, melee weapons and molotovs. Small isometric figures; blood marks on the street, no dismemberment or close-up gore.
- **General mature content:** Yes. The game is about organized crime: drug dealing (the Trap House), shakedowns of businesses, gang warfare and fights with police. It depicts homelessness and drug addiction, a strip club and massage parlor as businesses, and street prostitution (women working a sidewalk and talking to drivers who pull over).
- **Some nudity or sexual content:** No. Nothing sexual is shown; all characters are fully clothed and the Stroll is only implied.
- **Adult only sexual content:** No.
